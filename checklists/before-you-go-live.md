# Before you go live

Claude Code can check a lot. These are the things you check yourself, with your own eyes, before anyone else sees the site.

## The facts
- [ ] The business name is spelled exactly right, everywhere
- [ ] The phone number is right. Call it from the site on your phone and make sure it rings the right place
- [ ] The address (if there is one) is right, and matches the Google Business Profile
- [ ] Nothing on the site is made up: no invented reviews, years in business, licences or prices
- [ ] Every placeholder image has been replaced or removed

## On your phone
- [ ] The page loads and nothing is cut off or overlapping
- [ ] The phone number is easy to find and tap
- [ ] Every button and link goes where it says
- [ ] Text is big enough to read without zooming

## The basics Google looks at
- [ ] Each page has one main heading that says what the business does and where
- [ ] Each page has its own title and description (Claude Code can show you them)
- [ ] There's a clear way to get in touch on every page

## Before you call it finished
- [ ] Someone who isn't you has looked at it on their phone
- [ ] You've saved a copy of the folder somewhere safe
