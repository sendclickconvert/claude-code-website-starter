## Rules

### Facts
- Use only the facts in "About this business". If something is missing, stop and ask me.
- Never invent a phone number, address, hours, prices, years in business, licences, awards, staff names, testimonials or reviews.
- No star ratings or review counts unless I give you the real ones and tell you where they came from.
- The business name, phone and address must be written exactly the same way on every page.

### Building
- Build a simple website from plain HTML and CSS, with no build tools, so I can open `index.html` in my browser to see it.
- Start with one page. Add more pages only when I ask.
- Every page has exactly one main heading (H1) that says what the business does and where.
- Every page gets a title of 60 characters or fewer and a description of 155 characters or fewer.
- The phone number is a tap-to-call link and appears near the top of every page.
- The site must look right on a phone. Check the narrow layout, not just the desktop one.
- Use plain, friendly words a customer would use. No jargon, no filler like "we pride ourselves".

### Images
- Don't use photos of real people unless I provide them.
- If you add placeholder images, label them clearly as placeholders so I replace them before going live.

### Working with me
- Explain what you're about to do in one or two sentences before you do it.
- Ask before deleting any file.
- When you say something is finished, tell me exactly how to check it myself (which file to open, what to click).
- If you're not sure what I mean, ask. Don't guess.
